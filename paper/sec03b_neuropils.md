# 3b. Results Ib: Stratification by Brain Region

The whole-brain census averages over very different tissue. Stage B repeats the analysis inside each neuropil, using the induced subgraph of neurons whose primary neuropil it is. Of the 78 neuropils, 51 had at least 200 neurons and were tested. Each used 25 degree-preserving nulls and 25 density-matched nulls, a smaller number than the whole-brain analysis because of the number of tests. Neurons were assigned to the neuropil holding most of their pre- and post-synaptic counts, and an independent streaming recomputation reproduced the assignment of all 134,181 neurons exactly. Neuropils were grouped, before any test, into 15 sensory, 27 associative and 9 motor regions from published nomenclature; borderline regions (GNG, SAD, AOTU, AMMC, WED) were classified once and not revisited.

## 3b.1 Which structures are universal
Five triad classes behave the same way in essentially every region. Against the degree-preserving null, the empty triad (003) and the mutual pair (102) are enriched in all 51 neuropils (medians z = +45.9 and +47.1), and the single-edge triad (012) is depleted in all 51 (median z = -46.5). Against the density-matched null, 003 and 012 again hold in all 51, and 102 holds in 50. The open-chain classes are depleted in 47 to 49 neuropils under the degree-preserving null (021D 49, 021C 48, 021U 47), with medians between -33.4 and -27.0.

The density-matched null is more permissive for chains. Under it, 021U is enriched in 50 regions (median z = +281.7), 021D in 39, and 021C is depleted in 33 and enriched in 18. A chain that looks suppressed against a degree-matched expectation can look common against a density-matched one, because the density-matched graph has no hubs. This is why we require a structure to survive all nulls before calling it confirmatory, and why the chain result is stated against the degree-preserving null.

The classes with a closed or reciprocated component behave similarly across regions. The two classes with one mutual pair and one extra edge (111D, 111U) are enriched in 45 regions each, and the dense classes 201, 210 and 300 are enriched in 50, 39 and 41 regions under the degree-preserving null. Their whole-brain values are mixing-unresolved (Section 2.3), so we cite them only as context.

## 3b.2 The feedforward loop is heterogeneous
The feedforward loop (030T), enriched over the whole brain, splits across regions. It is enriched (z above 3) in 21 neuropils, depleted (z below -3) in 17, and near zero in the remaining 13; the median z is only +1.1. Enrichment is largest in the optic lobe medulla (ME_L z = +204.8, ME_R +189.7, in 28,049 and 28,856 neurons) and in the gnathal ganglia (GNG +200.1, 5,335 neurons), and smaller in the left lamina (LA_L +48.7), the left inferior posterior slope (IPS_L +30.8) and the superior medial protocerebrum (SMP_R +21.3, SMP_L +21.0). It is depleted in the ellipsoid body (EB z = -50.5), both antennal lobes (AL_R -24.2, AL_L -24.5), the posterior ventrolateral protocerebrum (PVLP_R -13.6, PVLP_L -11.0), and the right anterior optic tubercle (AOTU_R -12.2). The two largest enriched regions carry most of the whole-brain signal. The feedforward loop is therefore a feature of the visual and gnathal circuits, not a brain-wide rule, and we state it that way.

## 3b.3 The cyclic triad
The cyclic triad (030C) is depleted in 36 neuropils and enriched in 2 under the degree-preserving null (median z = -6.9), and under the density-matched null it is split (enriched in 24, depleted in 13, median z = +0.8). So the cyclic triad is not enriched inside regions. The whole-brain enrichment against the first two nulls therefore comes from the way regions are composed, which the compartment-preserving null removes. It reverses to -65 against that null (Section 3.3).

## 3b.4 The compartment null within regions
Applying the compartment-preserving null inside each region is a harder test, because within a single neuropil the endpoint labels it preserves are mostly uniform, and the null can become degenerate (the median region has 39 distinct rewiring groups, and 3 have 3 or fewer). We report it as Tier 2 for that reason. Of the region-class pairs compared, 162 agree in sign with the degree-preserving result and 9 flip. All nine flips are for the single-edge class (012), in AMMC_R, CRE_R, LA_L, LA_R, MB_CA_L, MB_CA_R, MB_VL_R, OCG and SIP_R. The results file marks the flips as the informative minority. The other 162 pairs agree, so for most region-class pairs the compartment null reproduces the direction found with the degree-preserving null.

## 3b.5 Do the profiles differ by functional class?
Structure that is universal cannot distinguish regions, so we tested directly whether enrichment profiles differ between sensory, associative and motor regions. The preregistered test was a permutation test on class labels (10,000 permutations, between-minus-within statistic on the profile of z-scores), and its result was p = 0.299. We therefore make no claim that motif profiles differ by functional class. Post-hoc single-class tests, run after seeing the feedforward-loop split, were also non-significant (030T p = 0.074, 030C p = 0.121, 102 p = 0.130, 003 p = 0.224). The regional variation in 030T described above is real in the sense that individual regions differ from the null by large margins. It does not line up with the three-way functional classification, which is a coarse partition that groups the medulla with several regions where the loop is depleted.
